## Battlecross Privacy Policy

### 1. Introduction

Protecting your privacy matters to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information is handled when you play
Battlecross, our mobile game, and who is responsible for it.

### 2. What stays on your device

Your game progress (hero, items, settings, saves) is stored locally on your device. We have no access to it. If you
delete the app, the local data is removed with it.

### 3. Online leaderboard

If you play online, the game sends your score, the nickname you chose, and a random player ID generated by the game to
our leaderboard server (hosted on Cloudflare). The player ID is not linked to your name, email address, device or
account. We do not collect your IP address for the leaderboard beyond what the hosting provider logs technically. Do not
use your real name as a nickname.

### 4. Advertising

Battlecross is free and shows ads (rewarded videos and interstitials) through Unity LevelPlay, which mediates ad
networks including Google AdMob, Unity Ads and ironSource. To show ads, these providers may collect and process your
device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and ad interaction data, and may use it to show and
measure ads. Battlecross is not directed to children.

- **Consent:** where required by law (for example in the EEA, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), the game asks for
  your consent through Google's consent form before ads are loaded. You can reopen it in **Options → Privacy settings**.
- **Advertising ID:** you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalisation, in your device
  settings (Android: Settings → Google → Ads; iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking).
- **Providers' policies:** see the privacy policies of Unity (unity.com/legal/privacy-policy), Google
  (policies.google.com/privacy) and ironSource (is.com/privacy-policy).

### 5. What we do not collect

We do not use analytics tools such as Google Analytics or Firebase, we do not ask for an account, and we do not access
your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or location.

### 6. Children

Battlecross is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you
believe a child has provided personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

### 7. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict and port your data, to object to processing and
to withdraw consent at any time. Because we hold no account data, the only data we could look up is a leaderboard
entry; send us your nickname and we will delete it on request. You may also complain to your data protection authority.
